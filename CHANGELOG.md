# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First release.

- A Legacy button on the world map counts the unfinished Legacy objectives on the map you're viewing. Its menu tracks them or opens them in the Legacy panel.
- Pins mark 493 undiscovered areas and dungeon entrances, with the challenge and its Legacy points in the tooltip.
- Challenges with no fixed location are listed separately.
- `/lh audit` checks the bundled data against the game.
- Data from Forever build 1.60.1.69913.
