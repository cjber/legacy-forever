### Changed

- **The dungeon and raid entrance row says what it does.** Turning "Show dungeon and raid entrances" off hides Legacy's pins on entrances, in the World map settings and in the Legacy map menu alike. The menu still lists them.
- **Fewer settings pages.** The Updates page is gone: "What's new after an update" sits on the main Legacy Forever page under the three pages, World map, Objective tracker and What counts.
- **A clearer name for the map badge row.** "On the world map" is now "Zone completion on the map", matching "Zone completion in the tracker".

### Removed

- **The "Attach to quest tracker" switch.** The Forever tracker column always sits beside the game's tracker now, so the switch did nothing. "Lock the tracker" stays.
- **The Forever addons page and the suggestions to install my other addons.** The page, its "Suggest my other Forever addons" switch (settings and map menu), and the grey lines on pins and zone completion tooltips are gone.
